Last updated: November 18, 2026

1. What this is

Launch Radar is a personal tool used only by its owner to publish Shorts to the owner's own YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public and has no user accounts.

2. YouTube Terms

The tool uses YouTube API Services. Use of the tool, and every video it uploads, is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and Community Guidelines. See also the Google Privacy Policy and our Privacy Policy.

3. Content and credit

  • Uploads are short excerpts of public gameplay clips, edited into a vertical format with captions.
  • The original creator is credited on screen and in the description of every upload.
  • Content is reviewed before upload to keep it within YouTube's Community Guidelines.

4. Removal requests

Any creator who does not want their clip used can request removal by opening an issue at github.com/jessicowhite/launch-radar-site/issues. Valid requests are honored and the video is removed. Copyright notices can also be sent through YouTube's standard copyright process.

5. No warranty

The tool and this site are provided as is, without warranty of any kind.
