Launch Radar

A personal publishing tool for one GTAVI clips channel.

Launch Radar is a single-user desktop tool run by the owner of the YouTube channel @gta6clipsshd. It finds popular public GTAVI gameplay clips, formats them as vertical Shorts with on-screen credit to the original creator, and, after an editorial review step, uploads them to the owner's own YouTube channel.

How it uses YouTube API Services

  • The channel owner signs in once with Google OAuth (scope: youtube.upload).
  • videos.insert uploads a finished Short to the owner's channel, with a title, description and creator credit.
  • No other users, no public sign-up, no access to anyone else's account or data.

Credit to creators

Every upload names the original streamer on screen and in the description, with a link to their channel. Creators can ask for any clip to be removed and it will be taken down. See the Terms.
